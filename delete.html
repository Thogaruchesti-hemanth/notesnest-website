Account Deletion

Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All data associated with this account will be purged from our active systems.

Pre-Deletion Checklist

Back up all your local data
Export important notes and files
Cancel all active subscriptions
Leave or transfer shared workspaces

What will be deleted?

Personal Information
All Saved Notes
Tasks & Reminders
App Settings
Integration Tokens
Custom Themes

Data Retention Policy

We retain your data for a grace period of 30 days to comply with legal obligations. After this period, your data is scrubbed from our primary and backup servers.

Reactivation Policy

Once the deletion process is finalized, account reactivation is not available. You must create a new account to use NotesNest again.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to erasure. By initiating this process, you are exercising your right to be forgotten within our infrastructure.

How to proceed

Method 1 Recommended

Delete via App

Instant processing through your account dashboard.

  1. Navigate to Settings
  2. Select "Privacy & Security"
  3. Click "Delete My Account"
  4. Verify identity via MFA
Open App Dashboard
