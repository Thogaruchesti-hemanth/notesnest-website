Privacy Policy

Your data security is our priority. This policy outlines how NotesNest manages your information while you build your second brain.

Last Updated: June 4, 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The type of information we collect depends on how you use NotesNest.

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.

Your Content

We securely store the notes, images, and attachments you create within the app for cross-device syncing.

Usage Data

Log information, device type, operating system, and app interaction metrics help us debug and optimize performance.

Ads Policy

To keep NotesNest free for everyone, we may display limited advertisements. Our commitment to your privacy remains absolute.

Non-Personalized Ads

Ads are served based on contextual factors (like app version or device type) rather than your personal note content or identity.

Strict Opt-Out

Premium subscribers experience a completely ad-free workspace. We never use your note data to train ad-targeting algorithms.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

Providing cross-platform synchronization.
Managing user accounts and support.
Analyzing anonymous usage patterns to improve UI/UX.

3. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS for data in transit.

Zero-Sell

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Encryption

Pin-locked options & encryption for vault notes.

Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

Your Data Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your personal data. You can also export your notes in markdown format at any time directly through the application settings. For account deletion requests, please contact our support team.
