NotesNest User Guide

Welcome to NotesNest! This comprehensive guide will help you master all features and get the most out of your productivity companion. Optimize your workflow today.

Version: 3.2.2 Updated: June 2, 2026 Recommended: Android 10+

Getting Started

Installation

  • 1
    Download from Google Play: Visit the NotesNest page on Google Play Store and tap "Install".
  • 2
    Open the App: Once installed, tap "Open" or find the NotesNest icon in your app drawer.
  • 3
    Create Account or Sign In: Choose to create a new account with email/password or use Google Sign-In.

Email Sign-Up

Enter your email, create a strong password (8+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols), and choose a username.

Google Sign-In

Tap "Sign in with Google" for quick access using your existing Google account. Your name, email, and profile picture will be imported.

Pro Tip

Use a strong, unique password for your account. NotesNest uses encrypted storage to protect your credentials—we never store passwords in plain text.

Managing Notes

Creating a New Note

Tap the 'Create' Button

Located at the bottom-right corner of the main screen.

Add Title

Enter a descriptive title for your note.

Write Content

Type your note content in the main text area.

Editing Notes

Tap any note from your list to open and edit it. Changes are saved automatically in real-time.

Deleting Notes

Swipe left on a note or use the menu option within the note to move it to trash.

View Options

Switch between List View and Grid View for optimal organization based on your preference.

Notes Pinning

Pin your most important notes to the top of the list for quick access. Pinned notes stay at the top of your screen, ensuring you never lose track of critical tasks or information.

Exporting Content
